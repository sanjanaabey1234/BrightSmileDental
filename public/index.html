How it works

Three easy steps to know where you stand

No forms and no waiting room. Just tap your answers.

  1. 01

    Chat with Brighty

    Tap simple answers about your lips, tongue, gums, saliva, teeth, dentures, cleanliness and any pain.

  2. 02

    Get your result instantly

    Each area is scored 0 (healthy), 1 (changes) or 2 (unhealthy). See what may be going on, how urgent it is and a personal self-care plan.

  3. 03

    We help you follow up

    Your answers go to our dental team. Request an appointment in one tap and we'll contact you to confirm.

What we check

The 8 areas of a standard oral health check

Tap any area to see what healthy looks like, and when to get help.

LipsColour, moisture, sores
  • 0HealthySmooth, pink and moist
  • 1ChangesDry, chapped or red corners
  • 2See a dentistSwelling, ulcers or bleeding
TongueColour, coating, patches
  • 0HealthyNormal, pink and moist
  • 1ChangesPatchy, grooved or lightly coated
  • 2See a dentistBright red, white patches, ulcers or swelling
Gums & tissuesBleeding, redness, swelling
  • 0HealthyPink, firm and no bleeding
  • 1ChangesRed, puffy, sore spots or bleed when brushing
  • 2See a dentistBleed on their own, deep ulcers or widespread redness
SalivaDry or sticky mouth
  • 0HealthyMoist and flowing normally
  • 1ChangesDry or sticky
  • 2See a dentistVery dry, thick or stringy, hard to swallow
Natural teethDecay, breaks, wear
  • 0HealthyNo decayed or broken teeth
  • 1Changes1 to 3 decayed, broken or worn teeth
  • 2See a dentist4 or more, or fewer than 4 teeth left
DenturesFit, damage, comfort
  • 0HealthyIntact, fit well, worn comfortably
  • 1ChangesLoose, small chip or only worn a little
  • 2See a dentistBroken, missing pieces or too painful to wear
CleanlinessPlaque, food, breath
  • 0HealthyClean, no food or build-up
  • 1ChangesSome plaque or mild bad breath
  • 2See a dentistHeavy plaque, food debris or strong bad breath
Dental painAches, sensitivity
  • 0HealthyNo pain or discomfort
  • 1ChangesMild pain, avoiding chewing on one side
  • 2See a dentistSevere pain, can't eat or sleep
Your result

Clear, colour-coded next steps

Your result follows the standard OHAT action thresholds, so you always know how soon to act.

Healthy

All areas score 0

Keep up daily care: brush twice with fluoride toothpaste and clean between your teeth.

Self-care + check-up

Any area scores 1

Follow your enhanced self-care plan and book a routine check-up within 2-4 weeks.

See a dentist soon

Any area scores 2

Book a dental visit within 24-72 hours, especially for bleeding, severe pain or ulcers.

Emergency

Red-flag symptoms

Spreading swelling or trouble breathing or swallowing: get emergency care straight away.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can't find your answer? Ask Brighty, our assistant, in the chat.

Is the self-check really free?

Yes. It's free, takes about 3 minutes and you don't need to create an account.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It's a screening tool based on the Oral Health Assessment Tool (OHAT). It shows what might be going on and how urgent it is. Only a dentist can diagnose after examining you.

Who sees my answers?

Your answers are saved for our dental team so they can follow up with you. They are not shared publicly.

Can I do the check for someone else?

Yes. Choose "Someone I care for" at the start. It works well for children, older parents or people in residential care.

What if it's an emergency?

The check asks about emergency signs first. If there is spreading facial swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing, or heavy bleeding, call 1990 or go to the nearest emergency department straight away.

Can I see my results again later?

Yes. On the same device your chat and results are kept, and you can print or save them as a PDF.
