How the self-check works

1

Answer simple questions

Tap answers about your lips, tongue, gums, saliva, teeth, dentures, cleanliness and pain - the 8 areas of the OHAT.

2

Get an instant result

Each area is scored 0 (healthy), 1 (changes) or 2 (unhealthy). You see likely causes, a priority level and a personal self-care plan.

3

We follow up

Your results go securely to our dental team. Request an appointment in one tap, and we will call you to confirm.

All scores 0

Keep up daily care: brush twice with fluoride toothpaste and clean between teeth.

Any score of 1

Enhanced self-care: adjusted brushing, alcohol-free rinse, moisturisers - plus a check-up.

Any score of 2

Prompt dental referral - especially for bleeding, severe pain, broken roots or ulcers.

Red-flag symptoms

Spreading swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing: emergency care immediately.
