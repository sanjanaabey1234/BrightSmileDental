How to use SELF DOC

Watch the short video, or follow the simple steps below. It works the same on the website and in the Android app.

  • About 3 minutes
  • Captions in English

Chapters

The video is in English, with captions on screen.

Step by step

Four simple steps

Each step has a button to watch that part of the video.

  1. 1

    Take the free check

    Start the free check and answer short questions about your lips, tongue, gums, teeth and any pain. Most answers take one tap. At the end you get a clear result and what to do next.

    Start the free check
  2. 2

    Book a visit

    Choose why you are coming, a day and a time of day, then add your name and phone number. The clinic calls or messages you to confirm.

    Book a visit
  3. 3

    Read the blog

    Tips and news from the dental team. Search or pick a category, open an article and tap a photo to see it full screen.

    Open the blog
  4. 4

    Use the Android app

    The app shows the same website. Use the bar at the bottom: Home, Free check, Blog and Book. The labels follow the language you choose.
